## Name
Multicell: Focus Cell for Google Sheets

## Summary (132 characters max)
Highlights the row and column of your active cell or selection in Google Sheets. Toggle with one click or a shortcut.

## Category
Workflow & Planning (or Productivity → Tools)

## Description
Lose track of which row you're on in a wide spreadsheet? Multicell draws a soft highlight across the row and column of your selected cell in Google Sheets, so your eyes can follow the data.

Features
• Highlights the full row and column of the active cell
• Optionally follows multi-cell selections (highlights every selected row and column)
• Pick any highlight color and adjust its opacity with a slider, with a live preview in your sheet
• Turn it on or off from the toolbar or with a keyboard shortcut (Alt+Shift+Q by default, changeable at chrome://extensions/shortcuts)
• Settings sync across your Chrome browsers
• Works with frozen rows and columns, scrolling and sheet tabs

Privacy
Multicell never reads your spreadsheet contents and makes no network requests. It only needs access to Google Sheets pages and its own settings storage.

Multicell is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

## Single purpose (privacy tab)
Highlight the row and column of the selected cell in Google Sheets to make spreadsheets easier to read.

## Permission justifications (privacy tab)
- **storage**: Saves the user's settings (on/off, follow selection, color, opacity) so they persist across tabs and restarts.
- **Host access to https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/***: The content script must run on Google Sheets pages to read the position of the selection outline and draw the highlight.
